Science Olympiad robot vehicle

Directed 6+ prototype iterations of a competition robot as Build Event Director, combining brushed/stepper motors, encoders, and ultrasonic sensors on an Arduino/ESP32 stack, machined and printed in-house.

Aerospace

High-Power Rocket Rover and Deployment System

I designed the 3D-printed rover for our high-power rocket (4700 ft apogee) that competed in the NASA Student Launch Challenge. The rover, which possesses remote controlability, a live FPV feed, and various environmental sensors, deploys from an 80/20 aluminum guide rail at a set altitude and lands via parachute.
