Background

Design, analysis, and a habit of taking things apart to see how they work.

I'm a first-year engineering student at Purdue intending to major in mechanical engineering. I recently joined Purdue's Formula SAE team as a suspension team member and I am excited to develop my skills.

Some highlights from my prior experience are placing 3RD in Engineering CAD at Science Olympiad Nationals and passing the PDR, CDR, AND FRR to launch our high-power rocket at NASA Student Launch.

In my free time, I enjoy a LOT of hobbies, which include running, photography, video-editing, pickleball, hiking, gaming, and more. The list is always growing!
